About Us 🐾

Life Care Animal Welfare is a community-driven NGO founded with one simple belief: Every life matters. Based in Chhatrapati Sambhajinagar, we dedicate our time, love, and energy to rescuing, feeding, and rehabilitating injured and stray animals.

“Since 2017, we’ve rescued 1000+ animals, hosted free vaccination camps, and raised awareness about kindness.”

Our journey began when Jayesh Shinde saw a helpless puppy left on the street. That moment turned into a mission — building a compassionate team of volunteers committed to making a difference.

Change starts small — with a bowl of food, a gentle hand, or a safe shelter. Together, we can be the voice for the voiceless. 💖
